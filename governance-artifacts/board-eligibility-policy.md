# Board Eligibility Policy (DRAFT)

<mark style="color:red;">**THE FOLLOWING CONTENT IS DRAFT AND NOT FORMALIZED.**</mark>

## Purpose

The Articles of Association ("Articles") and the Bylaws of the Ayra Association ("Bylaws") establish how Directors are nominated, elected, and removed. They do not, however, state who is eligible to be nominated, how eligibility is verified, or what happens when a Director's circumstances change part way through a term. Several provisions operate automatically but depend on terms that are never defined.

This policy closes those gaps. It states plainly what the Articles and Bylaws leave implicit, so that nominations, elections, and mid-term changes are handled consistently and predictably.

This policy interprets and supplements the Articles and the Bylaws. It does not amend them. Where this policy and the Articles or the Bylaws conflict, the Articles and the Bylaws prevail.

## Authority

The Articles make the Governing Board responsible for the "adoption of the Association's policies, procedures or governing rules other than these Articles or the Bylaws and the amendments thereto." This policy is adopted by the Governing Board under that authority.

The Bylaws already use this pattern for one Advisory Council: nominees to the Ambassador Advisory Council must meet stated qualification criteria, the Secretary determines whether a nominee qualifies, the Secretary confers with the Nominating Committee where qualification is in question, and a Governing Board decision on qualification is final. This policy extends that same mechanism to all Advisory Councils and to Director nominations generally.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- Nomination of candidates for the Governing Board by the Advisory Councils
- Election of Directors by the Members' Assembly
- Continuation of a Director in office during a term
- Determination and filling of Board vacancies

This policy does not apply to the Executive Director, who serves as a non-voting *ex-officio* Director and chairs the Governing Board under the Bylaws, and who may not be an employee, officer, director, or consultant of any Member.

## Defined Terms

The Bylaws refer to a Director employed by a Member who is "in Default or Dues Delinquent." Neither term is defined anywhere in the Articles or the Bylaws. The following definitions fill that gap and are anchored to the membership termination mechanism the Bylaws already establish, so that no new obligation is created.

**Member** means the legal entity, or in the case of an Individual Member the natural person, admitted to membership of the Ayra Association in a membership class.

**Affiliate Group** has the meaning given in the Bylaws.

**Annual Membership Fee** has the meaning given in the Bylaws, and includes other dues and any compounded late fees owed by a Member.

**Dues Delinquent** means a Member whose Annual Membership Fee remains unpaid after the invoice due date, and who has not yet been deemed to have resigned under the Bylaws. A Member becomes Dues Delinquent on the day after the invoice due date and ceases to be Dues Delinquent on payment in full.

**Default** means a Member that is in material breach of its Membership Agreement, the Bylaws, or any other Controlled Document, and that has not cured the breach within thirty (30) calendar days of receipt of written notice of the breach from the Ayra Association.

**Good Standing** means a Member that, at the time the determination is made:

- Has been admitted to a membership class and has a Membership Agreement in force
- Is neither Dues Delinquent nor in Default
- Has not given notice of withdrawal from membership that has not yet taken effect
- Is not the subject of a pending expulsion proceeding before the Governing Board

A Member whose Annual Membership Fee for the current period has not yet been invoiced, or has been invoiced but is not yet past its due date, is in Good Standing.

**Affiliated** means, in relation to a nominee or Director and a Member, that the person is an employee, officer, director, partner, or the designated Member Representative of that Member or of a legal entity within that Member's Affiliate Group. An Individual Member is Affiliated with itself.

## Eligibility to be Nominated

The Bylaws provide that Directors are elected by the Members' Assembly from among candidates nominated by the Advisory Councils, with seats allocated by membership class. They do not state what makes a candidate eligible. The following criteria apply.

A candidate is eligible to be nominated for a Board seat allocated to a membership class only if, on the nomination deadline for that election:

1. The candidate is a natural person.
2. The candidate is Affiliated with a Member that is in Good Standing and that belongs to the membership class to which the seat is allocated.
3. No other Director then in office, and no other candidate nominated in the same election, is Affiliated with the same Affiliate Group. The Bylaws allocate Board seats to represent membership classes, and permit a single Affiliate Group to hold more than one Executing Membership. Absent this limit, one Affiliate Group could hold multiple seats and the representational basis of the Board would be defeated.
4. The candidate is not an employee, officer, director, or consultant of the Ayra Association.
5. In the case of a seat allocated to Individual Members, the candidate additionally meets the Ambassador Advisory Council qualification criteria set out in the Bylaws. This policy does not alter those criteria.

A nominating Member shall confirm in writing, at the time of nomination, that it intends to maintain its membership through the term of office for which its candidate is nominated. This confirmation is a statement of intent. It is not a payment obligation, and it does not require payment of any Annual Membership Fee in advance of its due date.

## Verification of Eligibility

The Secretary shall determine whether each candidate meets the eligibility criteria in this policy. Where eligibility is in question, the Secretary shall confer with the Nominating Committee. A decision of the Governing Board on a candidate's eligibility is final.

Eligibility shall be verified at two points:

- On the nomination deadline for the election
- On the date of the Members' Assembly at which the election is held

A candidate who is eligible on the nomination deadline but not on the date of the Members' Assembly is not eligible for election. The Secretary shall notify the candidate, the nominating Member, and the relevant Advisory Council as soon as the Secretary becomes aware of the change, so that the Advisory Council has the opportunity to nominate an alternative candidate before the election.

The Secretary shall maintain a record of each eligibility determination.

## Continuation in Office

The Bylaws provide that a Director is deemed to have resigned with immediate effect on termination of the membership of that Director's Member legal entity, and that a Director employed by a Member in Default or Dues Delinquent, where that implies the termination of its membership, is deemed to have resigned without further action by the Members' Assembly. Both provisions operate automatically. The following clarifies how they are applied.

### Termination of the Member's membership

Where a Member's membership ends, a Director Affiliated with that Member is deemed to have resigned with effect from the date the membership ends as determined under the Bylaws.

Being Dues Delinquent does not by itself vacate a seat. A seat is vacated on this ground only once the Member has actually been deemed to have resigned under the Bylaws. That outcome depends on the Secretary having issued the payment reminder the Bylaws require. Where no such reminder was issued, the Member has not been deemed to have resigned, and the seat is not vacated. The Secretary shall confirm that the required reminder was issued before treating a seat as vacated on this ground.

### A Director ceasing to be Affiliated

The Articles and the Bylaws are silent on what happens when a Director remains willing to serve but ceases to be Affiliated with the Member through whose class the seat was filled, for example on changing employer. The seat is vacated, unless:

- The Director becomes Affiliated with another Member that is in Good Standing and belongs to the same membership class as the seat, and
- The Advisory Council for that membership class reconfirms the Director within three (3) weeks, and
- The Governing Board confirms that the eligibility criteria in this policy continue to be met

A Director elected to a seat allocated to Individual Members holds that membership personally, and this section does not apply to that Director.

### A Member changing membership class

Where a Member moves to a different membership class during a Director's term, a Director Affiliated with that Member continues in office for the balance of the term. The seat remains allocated to the membership class to which it was allocated at the time of election, and is filled from that class at the next election.

### Notification and replacement

The Secretary shall promptly notify the Governing Board, the affected Director, and the relevant Advisory Council when a seat is vacated under this section, and shall record the date of the vacancy.

The Bylaws require the Advisory Council for the membership class represented by a vacated seat to appoint a new Director within three (3) weeks of the vacancy, to serve for the balance of the vacating Director's term. The eligibility criteria in this policy, and the verification process in this policy, apply to that appointment.

## Limits of this Policy

This policy does not:

- Create any ground for revoking a Director that is not already set out in the Bylaws. Revocation of a Director remains reserved to the Members' Assembly on the grounds and by the majorities the Articles and the Bylaws require.
- Alter the Annual Membership Fee, the basis on which it is determined, the invoicing and payment timetable, or any other obligation of a Member. Those matters are governed by the Articles, the Bylaws, and the Membership Agreement.
- Require any Member to pay an Annual Membership Fee in advance of its due date as a condition of nominating a candidate or of its candidate being elected.
- Alter the qualification criteria for nominees to the Ambassador Advisory Council set out in the Bylaws.
- Alter the composition of the Governing Board or the allocation of seats among membership classes.

## Matters Requiring Amendment of the Bylaws

The following cannot be resolved by policy and are recorded here so that they can be addressed when the Bylaws are next amended:

- The maximum number of representatives on the Civil Society, Ecosystem, Ambassador, and Associate Advisory Councils is left unspecified in the Bylaws. Until those numbers are set, the size of the bodies that nominate Directors is undetermined.
- The terms "Default" and "Dues Delinquent" are used in the Bylaws but defined nowhere in them. The definitions in this policy fill the gap on an interim basis. They belong in the Bylaws.
- The Bylaws establish no eligibility requirement for a nominee at the time of nomination or election. This policy supplies one. A durable requirement belongs in the Bylaws.

## Review

This policy shall be reviewed by the Governing Board at least annually, and in any event before the opening of nominations for each election of Directors.
