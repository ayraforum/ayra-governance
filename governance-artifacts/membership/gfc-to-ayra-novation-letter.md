# GFC-to-Ayra Membership Novation and Transition Letter

## Document Control

- Status: Draft for legal review
- Type: Companion letter agreement for legacy GFC members transitioning to Ayra Association
- Related: Membership Agreement; Schedule A and Schedule B to the Membership Agreement; Ayra Governing Documents
- Use: Send with the Ayra Association Membership Agreement to members whose existing membership relationship is with GAN Formation Corporation

## Drafting Notes

- Purpose: a short letter that moves the member's membership from GFC to Ayra. Keep it simple — it is a transition, not a renegotiation.
- The legacy contracting entity is GAN Formation Corporation, a benefit company incorporated under the laws of British Columbia, Canada, defined as "GFC" on first use.
- Governing law is Swiss, to align with the Ayra Membership Agreement.
- This letter operates as a termination of the GFC relationship plus admission of the member to Ayra. Membership in a Swiss association (Verein) is non-transferable (Swiss Civil Code art. 70(2)) — membership is not assigned or transferred, it ends with GFC and is newly granted by Ayra on admission.
- Admission to Ayra is a Board function. The Board's Membership Committee pre-reviews and pre-approves each transitioning member; confirm that approval is recorded before sending.
- Ayra signs by its Secretary (Drummond Reed), separate from the GFC signatory (Darrell O'Donnell), to avoid one person binding both sides of the transition.
- The "Ayra does not assume GFC's past liabilities" point (section 4) is deliberate — it signals that pre-transition debts, unpaid amounts, and in-kind shortfalls stay with GFC. Members come to terms with GFC on any outstanding balance separately; that is not a condition of this letter and is not detailed in it.
- The current membership term runs through December 31, 2026 (section 5); confirm the term end per member. Dues already paid, credits, and adjustments are a renewal matter and are intentionally left out of this letter.

By signing below, the Parties agree:

1. [Member Legal Name]'s membership agreement with GFC ends, and [Member Legal Name] becomes a member of Ayra under the Ayra Association Membership Agreement and its Governing Documents (the "Ayra Membership Agreement").

2. Ayra is a Swiss association, so membership is not transferred or assigned — the GFC membership ends and Ayra grants membership on admission. The effect is continuous membership, now with Ayra.

3. This takes effect on the later of the effective date of the Ayra Membership Agreement and the date all Parties have signed.

4. This letter moves the membership only. Ayra does not take on, and is not responsible for, any past liability, debt, unpaid amount, in-kind shortfall, or other obligation arising under the GFC membership — those remain with GFC.

5. The membership term runs through December 31, 2026. Renewal — including any dues already paid, credit, or adjustment — will be dealt with at renewal, not in this letter.

6. This letter is governed by the laws of Switzerland, to match the Ayra Membership Agreement.
