# GFC-to-Ayra Membership Novation and Transition Letter

## Document Control

- Status: Draft for legal review
- Type: Companion letter agreement for legacy GFC members transitioning to Ayra Association
- Related: Membership Agreement; Schedule A and Schedule B to the Membership Agreement; Ayra Governing Documents
- Use: Send with the Ayra Association Membership Agreement to members whose existing membership relationship is with GAN Formation Corporation

## Drafting Notes

- The legacy contracting entity is GAN Formation Corporation, a benefit company incorporated under the laws of British Columbia, Canada, defined in this letter as "GFC" on first use and used consistently thereafter.
- Governing law is Swiss, to align with the Ayra Membership Agreement (see section 12).
- This letter operates as a termination of the GFC relationship plus admission of the member to Ayra. Membership in a Swiss association (Verein) is non-transferable (Swiss Civil Code art. 70(2)) — this applies to member organizations as well as individuals; membership is not assigned or transferred, it is ended with GFC and newly granted by Ayra on admission.
- Admission to Ayra is a Board function. The Board's Membership Committee pre-reviews and pre-approves each transitioning member before this letter is signed. Confirm the relevant approval is recorded before sending.
- Ayra signs by its Secretary (Drummond Reed), separate from the GFC signatory (Darrell O'Donnell), to avoid one person binding both sides of the transition.
- GFC members do not transition to Ayra without first coming to terms with GFC on any outstanding balance. Pre-transition debts are not assigned to or assumed by Ayra (see sections 5 and 6).
- Member-specific commercial terms, including membership class, membership term, dues, credit, and any outstanding balance or settlement, should be reflected in Schedule A and Schedule B to the Membership Agreement and, where useful, repeated in the cover email or member package.

This letter agreement confirms the transition of [Member Legal Name]'s membership relationship from GAN Formation Corporation, a benefit company incorporated under the laws of British Columbia, Canada ("GFC"), to Ayra Association ("Ayra") in connection with [Member Legal Name]'s execution of the Ayra Association Membership Agreement dated [date] (the "Ayra Membership Agreement").

For purposes of this letter agreement, GFC, Ayra, and [Member Legal Name] are each a "Party" and together are the "Parties".

## Background

A. [Member Legal Name] entered into a membership agreement with GFC dated [date of legacy agreement] (the "GFC Membership Agreement").

B. Ayra has been established as the successor organization to carry forward the long-term membership and governance structure contemplated for the Ayra community.

C. The Parties wish to transition [Member Legal Name]'s membership relationship from GFC to Ayra by terminating the GFC Membership Agreement and admitting [Member Legal Name] as a member of Ayra under the Ayra Membership Agreement, with the consent of GFC, Ayra, and [Member Legal Name].

D. Membership in Ayra, a Swiss association (Verein), is non-transferable and is granted by Ayra on admission; it is not transferred or assigned from GFC. The Parties intend this letter agreement to evidence their consent and, to the extent required, to operate as a novation under which the GFC Membership Agreement is terminated and replaced by [Member Legal Name]'s membership in Ayra on the terms set out below.

E. Ayra's admission of [Member Legal Name] has been pre-reviewed and pre-approved by the Membership Committee of the Ayra Board in accordance with Ayra's Governing Documents.

## Agreement

1. Transition Effective Date. The transition described in this letter agreement is effective as of the later of:

   (a) the effective date of the Ayra Membership Agreement; and

   (b) the date on which this letter agreement has been signed by all Parties,

   or such other date as the Parties may agree in writing (the "Transition Effective Date").

2. Novation: termination and admission. With effect from the Transition Effective Date, and by way of novation, the GFC Membership Agreement is terminated (as set out in section 3) and [Member Legal Name] is admitted as a member of Ayra. The Parties acknowledge that Ayra membership is not transferred or assigned from GFC; it is newly granted by Ayra on admission. From the Transition Effective Date, [Member Legal Name]'s membership rights and obligations will be governed by the Ayra Membership Agreement, including its Schedules, and by Ayra's Governing Documents as incorporated into that agreement.

3. Termination of GFC Membership Agreement. With effect from the Transition Effective Date, the GFC Membership Agreement is terminated and replaced by the Ayra Membership Agreement, except for any provisions of the GFC Membership Agreement that expressly survive termination and any accrued rights, obligations, claims, dues, credits, confidentiality obligations, payment obligations, or other matters that arose before the Transition Effective Date and have not been expressly settled, waived, assigned, assumed, or credited in writing.

4. Release of GFC from future membership obligations. [Member Legal Name] releases GFC from any obligation to provide membership rights, benefits, governance participation, or other future performance under the GFC Membership Agreement on or after the Transition Effective Date. GFC releases [Member Legal Name] from any future membership obligations under the GFC Membership Agreement on or after the Transition Effective Date, except as preserved under section 3 of this letter agreement.

5. Admission by Ayra; no assumption of legacy matters by default. Ayra admits [Member Legal Name] as a member of Ayra on the terms set out in the Ayra Membership Agreement, that admission having been pre-reviewed and pre-approved by the Membership Committee of the Ayra Board. As a default, Ayra does not assume any legacy GFC liability, obligation, receivable, refund obligation, disputed in-kind contribution, outstanding balance, or other pre-transition matter. The only exception is a matter that is expressly identified and assumed in Schedule A or Schedule B to the Ayra Membership Agreement, or in another written agreement signed by Ayra. Where no such matter is listed, Ayra assumes nothing.

6. Dues credit and outstanding amounts. Any credit for previously paid membership dues, and any adjusted dues payable to Ayra, will be determined and applied as set out in Schedule A and Schedule B to the Ayra Membership Agreement. Any outstanding amounts owed to GFC before the Transition Effective Date remain owed to GFC unless expressly assigned to Ayra, waived, credited, or otherwise settled in writing.

7. Confidential information and records. The Parties acknowledge that confidential information and records may have been provided to or generated by GFC and Ayra in connection with the membership relationship. From the Transition Effective Date, the treatment of confidential information in connection with Ayra membership will be governed by the Ayra Membership Agreement, without limiting any confidentiality obligation that survived under the GFC Membership Agreement.

8. No admission or waiver. This letter agreement is intended to complete an orderly transition of the membership relationship. It is not an admission of liability by any Party and does not waive any right, obligation, claim, defence, or remedy except as expressly stated in this letter agreement.

9. Further assurances. Each Party will sign and deliver any additional documents and take any reasonable steps necessary to give effect to this letter agreement and the transition of membership to Ayra.

10. Authority. Each person signing this letter agreement represents that they have authority to bind the Party on whose behalf they sign.

11. Counterparts and electronic signature. This letter agreement may be signed in counterparts. Signatures delivered electronically will be treated as originals for all purposes.

12. Governing law. This letter agreement is governed by the substantive laws of Switzerland, without regard to conflict of laws rules, to align with the Ayra Membership Agreement.

13. Entire transition agreement. This letter agreement, together with the Ayra Membership Agreement and its Schedules, contains the Parties' agreement regarding the transition of [Member Legal Name]'s membership from GFC to Ayra. If there is any conflict between this letter agreement and the Ayra Membership Agreement concerning [Member Legal Name]'s Ayra membership after the Transition Effective Date, the Ayra Membership Agreement will govern.
